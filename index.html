The Science of Living Food

Why eating living plants changes everything

Living Enzymes

Active enzymes that aid digestion and nutrient absorption are only present in living plants.

Hydrating Nutrients

Water-soluble vitamins (C, B complex) are at peak levels when plants are living and turgid.

Phytonutrient Density

Living microgreens contain up to 40x more nutrients than their mature counterparts.

Gut Health

Living plants deliver beneficial microbes and fiber that support microbiome diversity.

Our Mission

To make living, nutrient-dense food accessible to everyone

We believe that the revolution in nutrition isn't about supplements or processed foods—it's about returning to eating food the way nature intended: alive, fresh, and full of vital energy.

Our journey began when we discovered that most "fresh" produce has lost 50-80% of its nutrients within days of harvest. We asked: What if we could deliver food at its nutritional peak?

The answer was living microgreens. By delivering microgreens still growing in their soil, we ensure you get maximum nutrition, flavor, and vitality in every bite.

How Living Microgreens Work

From our farm to your table in under 24 hours

1

Seed to Sprout

We plant non-GMO, organic seeds in biodegradable trays with nutrient-rich soil.

2

Optimal Growth

Greens grow under full-spectrum LED lights with precise water and nutrient schedules.

3

Peak Nutrition Harvest

At 7-10 days, when nutrient density peaks, we prepare your order for delivery.

4

Delivered Living

Your living greens arrive in 100% recyclable packaging, ready to harvest at home.
